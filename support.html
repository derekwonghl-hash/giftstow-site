GiftStow Support

GiftStow is an offline gift-card organiser for iPhone.

Common questions

Where is my data stored?

Gift-card data is stored locally on your iPhone. GiftStow does not currently offer an account or cloud sync.

Why was a linear barcode not found in an imported image?

The current image-import path recognises QR codes. Use Scan barcode with camera for a live linear-barcode scan, or continue manually while retaining the selected image.

Why are reminders not appearing?

Check that notifications are allowed for GiftStow in iOS Settings and that the card has a valid future expiry date and reminder.

How do I remove a card?

Open the card, archive it if required, then use the permanent-delete action and confirm the warning.
