GiftStow Support

GiftStow is an offline gift-card organiser for iPhone.

Common questions

Where is my data stored?

Gift-card data is stored locally on your iPhone. GiftStow does not currently offer an account or cloud sync. Import up to 10 photos at a time and return to Pending imports on Wallet or Add card. Photos and confirmed corrections survive restarts, but drafts do not enter Wallet totals until you explicitly save each card.

Why was a linear barcode not found in an imported image?

The native GiftStow app can recognise supported linear barcodes and QR codes in imported images and suggest printed card details, entirely on your iPhone. Recognition is best-effort: a small, blurred or unsupported code may not be found. Compare the original image with the decoded preview and review every suggested value before saving. Image balances may be out of date; GiftStow does not fetch live balances or verify PINs. If detection fails, use Scan barcode with camera or continue manually while retaining the image. Expo Go and older development builds without the native recogniser support QR-only image detection.

Check in-store and optional online numbers/PINs separately. Only clearly labelled current balances are suggested; original values and handwritten corrections stay manual. If no barcode is decoded, GiftStow does not invent one for a new import.

Why are reminders not appearing?

Check that notifications are allowed for GiftStow in iOS Settings and that the card has a valid future expiry date and reminder.

How do I remove a card?

Open the card, archive it if required, then use the permanent-delete action and confirm the warning. GiftStow removes the card's database records and retries any pending protected-payload, imported-image and reminder cleanup when Wallet opens. Prefer in-app deletion before uninstalling: iOS protected storage can survive an uninstall, so removing the app is not a guaranteed protected-data wipe. Original photos in your Photos library are not removed.

Remove an unfinished import from Pending imports using Discard import. Draft cleanup retries safely; saved cards and original library photos are unchanged.

Still need help?

Email GiftStow support and describe what happened. Do not send real card numbers, PINs, readable barcodes or unredacted gift-card images.
