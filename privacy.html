GiftStow Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 July 2026

GiftStow is designed as a private, offline gift-card wallet. The initial release does not require an account and does not send your gift-card data to the developer or to a GiftStow server.

Information stored on your device

GiftStow may store information you choose to enter, including retailer names, card numbers, PINs, barcodes, balances, expiry dates, notes, activity history and approved card images. Card metadata and history are kept in the app’s local database. Sensitive card payloads use iOS protected storage, and approved images use the app’s local file storage.

Camera and photos

Camera access is used only when you choose to scan a gift-card barcode. Photo-library access is used only when you choose an image to import. Scanning and supported image recognition occur on the device. GiftStow does not upload these images or barcode values.

Notifications and device authentication

Expiry reminders are scheduled locally through iOS. If device authentication is enabled, GiftStow asks iOS to authenticate you; GiftStow does not receive or store your biometric data.

Data collection, advertising and tracking

The initial release does not include analytics, third-party advertising, cross-app tracking or a cloud backend. The developer therefore does not collect personal data through GiftStow.

Deleting your data

You can permanently delete a card from its card-detail screen. GiftStow then removes that card’s local metadata, protected payload, approved image and related activity. You can also remove the app using iOS. Contact support if you need help with local-data deletion.

Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated before a release adds any materially different data practice, such as cloud sync, diagnostics or real advertising.

Contact

For privacy questions, email giftstow.app@gmail.com.
